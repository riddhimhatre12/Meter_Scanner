Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Meter Scanner Pro

How do I scan my meter?
Navigate to the Scan page, allow camera access, centre the meter display in the frame with good lighting, and tap Capture. The AI will automatically detect and record the reading in seconds.
What if the scanner doesn't detect my meter?
Ensure the meter display is fully visible, well-lit, and free of shadows or glare. Try moving closer (15–25 cm). If it still fails, use the Manual Entry option on the Scan screen.
How is my electricity bill calculated?
Bills are calculated using Indian electricity slab rates (₹2.50–₹8.50 per unit) plus a meter rent of ₹25 and 18% GST. Visit the Bill Calculator for a detailed breakdown.
How do I export my reading data?
Go to the Readings page and click the Export CSV button. This downloads all your past readings with timestamps, OCR confidence, and notes into a spreadsheet-ready file.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is stored securely with encrypted passwords. Google OAuth uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0. We never store raw passwords and all connections use HTTPS in production.
